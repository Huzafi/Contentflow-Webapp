

# 🚀 CreatorFlow AI

https://postimg.cc/sv1HKmRj

**An AI-powered YouTube content creation assistant that transforms video topics into ready-to-use content ideas.**

CreatorFlow AI helps content creators simplify their YouTube planning workflow by generating titles, descriptions, thumbnail concepts, video hooks, outlines, and relevant tags from a single topic.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://contentflow-ai.puter.site/

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **YouTube Title Ideas** — Generate 5 title ideas based on your video topic.
- 📝 **Video Description** — Create an engaging YouTube description.
- 🖼️ **Thumbnail Ideas** — Generate 3 thumbnail concepts with suggested text.
- 🎬 **Intro & Hook** — Create a compelling video opening to capture viewers' attention.
- 📋 **Video Outline** — Generate a simple, structured video outline.
- 🔎 **Keywords & Tags** — Discover relevant keywords and tags for your content.
- 🔐 **Puter Authentication** — Sign in using your Puter account.
- 🎨 **Customizable Content** — Adjust the tone and target audience using the available options.
- 📱 **Responsive Dashboard** — Enjoy a clean, modern interface across different screen sizes.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Generation** — Leverage Puter.js AI services to generate content.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Open CreatorFlow AI.
2. Sign in with your Puter account.
3. Enter your YouTube video topic.
4. Select your preferred tone and target audience.
5. Click **Generate Content**.
6. Review the generated titles, description, thumbnail ideas, hook, outline, and tags.
7. Use the generated ideas to plan your next YouTube video.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of CreatorFlow AI is to make YouTube content planning faster, simpler, and more accessible by bringing essential content creation tools into a single AI-powered application.

Instead of brainstorming every content element separately, creators can start with one topic and generate a structured set of ideas to support their video production workflow.
